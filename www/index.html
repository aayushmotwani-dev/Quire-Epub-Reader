          Backup

          Everything in Quire lives on this device. A backup is one file with your books, your place in each, highlights, notes, goals, stats and settings. Keep it somewhere safe, or use it to move to another device.

          Without the books the file is tiny, but it can only restore onto a device that already has the same books.
